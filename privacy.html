Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Browsing vs. Uploading

Viewing: Anyone can browse and view the class gallery freely without signing in or sharing any personal information.

Uploading: To keep our gallery safe and accountable, only signed-in classmates can upload photos. When you sign in using Google via Supabase Auth, we only access your basic profile details (name, email address, and profile picture) along with the photos you upload.

2. How We Use Your Data

We do not use your personal data for tracking, ads, or anything external. Your Google login info is strictly used for:

  • Safety & Accountability: Knowing who uploaded a photo in case an issue occurs.
  • Attribution: Showing who shared each moment in the gallery.

3. Data Storage

Your account details and uploaded photos are securely stored using Supabase with access controls in place. We never sell or share your data with third parties.

4. Photo Removal & Contact

If you want a photo removed or your data deleted, you can use the Report button on the site anytime.

This project is built and maintained by your classmate, Muhammed Risvan—feel free to reach out directly with any questions or concerns.
