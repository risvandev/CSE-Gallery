Terms of Use

Last updated: November 2026

1. Keep it respectful

This gallery is a shared space for our class. Only upload photos that are appropriate and respectful to everyone in CSE-B.

2. Respect each other's privacy

Before uploading a photo featuring classmates, make sure it's something they wouldn't mind sharing here. Don't upload private or embarrassing photos meant to mock anyone.

3. Content removal

If you appear in a photo and want it taken down, or if something doesn't belong here, use the Report or Contact buttons on the site and it will be removed right away.

4. Your uploads

Only upload photos you have the right to share. By uploading to CSE-B Gallery, you agree to let classmates view them here.

5. 1-Minute delete window

If you upload the wrong photo by mistake, you can delete it directly from the gallery within 1 minute (60 seconds) of uploading. After that 1 minute passes, the delete option expires permanently so our shared class memories stay preserved together.

"If you need an older photo removed after the 1-minute window, just reach out using the Report or Contact buttons and we'll take it down for you."
